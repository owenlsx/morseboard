Privacy Policy

Your typing stays on your device.

Effective 6 September 2026

Overview

MorseBoard does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, accounts, or third-party software development kits.

Keyboard input

The MorseBoard keyboard processes taps locally on your device and inserts the resulting symbols or letters into the text field you are using. MorseBoard does not record typing history, read the surrounding contents of your documents, or send keyboard input to the developer or any third party. The keyboard does not request Full Access.

Settings and practice text

Keyboard preferences, such as layout, dot style, letter mode, capitalisation, and WPM, are stored locally in the keyboard extension’s settings on your device. They are not transmitted. The practice text in the companion app is held only while the app is running and is not saved by MorseBoard.

Data retention and deletion

Because MorseBoard does not collect personal data, the developer has no personal data to retain or delete. You can remove locally stored preferences by deleting MorseBoard from your device.

Children’s privacy

MorseBoard does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If MorseBoard’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the relevant feature is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email owenlsx.dev@gmail.com. You can also inspect MorseBoard’s public source code.
